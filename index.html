Fin & Flip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Published by Famz Games

Summary

Fin & Flip is a memory-matching game. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may use some device information as described below.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • Level progress and stars, coins, power-ups, and your daily-challenge streak
  • Your best Deep Dive score
  • Your settings (sound, vibration) and whether you have seen the tutorial

This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fin & Flip is free and supported by ads. We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, app version)
  • Information about how you interact with ads

Google acts under its own privacy practices. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices: on Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Google → Ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form (Google's consent tool) the first time you open it. You can review or change your choice later in the game under Settings → Privacy settings.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive personal information from the game. The only third party involved is Google, through its advertising services as described above, and Google Play, which handles downloads and updates under its own privacy policy.

Children

Fin & Flip is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information about you, we have no personal data to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google through advertising, you can use your Google account's privacy controls and the options in the section above. If you have a question about your privacy rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game or the law changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the game after an update means you accept the updated policy.
